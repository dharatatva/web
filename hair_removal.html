1. Is this really 100% painless compared to waxing?+
Yes, absolutely! Unlike waxing which forcefully pulls hair from the skin, this herbal powder softens and dissolves hair at the follicle level in 6-8 minutes. You simply wipe it away with a damp cloth without any pulling, tears, or pain.
2. How do I prepare and apply the powder?+
Take the required amount of powder in a bowl, mix it with normal water or rose water to form a smooth paste (neither too thick nor too watery). Apply evenly over the hair, keep it on for 6 to 8 minutes, and then wipe off using a damp towel in the opposite direction of hair growth.

No! Razor shaving and chemical creams cause friction and burns that darken the skin. Dharatatva Hair Removal Powder contains pure Chandan and Kasturi Haldi which actually help lighten underarm pigmentation and remove stubborn tanning.

Yes, it is gentle enough for underarms, hands, legs, stomach, back, and the bikini area. Avoid applying on broken skin, active cuts, or open sores.
5. How often can I use this product?+
You can use it whenever hair regrowth appears, typically every 10 to 15 days. With regular use, you will notice hair regrowth becomes much finer, softer, and slower.

No. Razors cut hair bluntly at an angle, which makes new hair feel prickly like stubble. Because our herbal formula softens the hair shaft, regrowth emerges naturally tapered, soft, and gentle to the touch.
7. Does it have that pungent chemical cream smell?+
No, we have infused natural sandalwood and floral botanicals that mask typical chemical odors, providing a much more pleasant, spa-like experience.
8. Should I perform a patch test before first use?+
Yes. As with any personal care product, we recommend doing a small patch test on your arm or leg 24 hours prior to full application to ensure skin compatibility.
9. Can men use this powder for body grooming?+
Yes, it works equally well on thicker, denser male body hair across the chest, back, arms, and legs.
