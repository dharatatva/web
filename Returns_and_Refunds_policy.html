Returns and Refunds

At Dharatatva, we take care to ensure that products are properly packed and dispatched. This Returns and Refunds Policy explains the circumstances under which products may be eligible for return, replacement, or refund.

1. Eligible Return / Replacement Situations

A product may be considered for replacement or other appropriate resolution where it is:

* Damaged during transit.
* Incorrect product received.
* Defective or materially damaged when received.
* Missing from the delivered package.
* Received in a condition that materially differs from the product ordered, subject to verification.

2. Products That May Not Be Eligible for Return

Due to hygiene, safety, product integrity, and the nature of certain products, opened or used products may generally not be eligible for return unless the product is defective, damaged, incorrect, or otherwise covered by applicable law or our policy.

This may include personal-care, skin-care, hair-care, wellness, ingestible, churna, nutrition, cream, wash, oil, and other products where return after opening may compromise hygiene or product integrity.

3. Reporting a Damaged or Incorrect Order

* Customers should contact Dharatatva Customer Support as soon as reasonably possible after receiving the order if a product is damaged, incorrect, defective, or otherwise eligible for a return or replacement.

* Customers may be requested to provide photographs or videos of the package, shipping label, product, seal, damage, or other relevant information to help us verify the issue.

4. Product Condition for Return

Where a return is approved, the product should generally be returned in the condition specified by Customer Support, along with the original packaging, labels, accessories, and other included items, wherever applicable.

5. Verification

* Returns, replacements, and refunds may be subject to verification.

* Dharatatva reserves the right to decline a return or replacement request where the reported issue is not covered by this policy or applicable law.

6. Refunds

* Where a refund is approved, the applicable refundable amount will be processed using the available refund method or, where appropriate, the original payment method.

* Refund timelines may vary depending on the payment gateway, bank, financial institution, or payment method.

7. Shipping Charges

* Treatment of shipping charges may depend on the reason for the return, the applicable policy, and applicable law.

* Where a product is confirmed to have been incorrectly supplied, damaged in transit, or otherwise covered for replacement, Dharatatva will determine the appropriate resolution in accordance with its applicable policy and law.

8. Refund Not Applicable for Change of Mind

Products that have been correctly delivered and are not eligible for return under the applicable product category or policy may not qualify for a refund solely because the customer has changed their mind, selected the wrong product, or no longer wishes to use the product, subject to applicable consumer rights and law.

9. Order Cancellation

Cancellation requests before dispatch are governed by our Cancellation Policy.

10. Contact Us

For return, replacement, or refund assistance, please contact:

Email: support@dharatatva.com

Important Note

Return and refund eligibility may vary depending on the product category, condition of the product, reason for return, order status, and applicable law. Please contact Customer Support before sending any product back.
