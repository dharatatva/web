Privacy Policy

At Dharatatva, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you visit our website, place an order, or interact with our services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may collect information such as:

* Name and contact details.
* Billing and delivery address.
* Mobile number and email address.
* Order and transaction-related information.
* Product preferences and customer support communications.
* Information voluntarily provided through forms, reviews, feedback, or other website interactions.

2. Payment Information

* Payments may be processed through authorised third-party payment gateways.

* Where payment information is handled directly by a payment service provider, such information may be subject to that provider's privacy and security practices.

* Dharatatva does not intentionally store complete payment-card information on its own systems where such information is processed directly by an authorised payment gateway.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

* Process and fulfil orders.
* Arrange delivery and shipment.
* Process payments and refunds.
* Provide customer support.
* Communicate regarding orders, products, or services.
* Improve our website, products, services, and customer experience.
* Prevent fraud, misuse, and unauthorised activity.
* Meet applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

4. Communications

* We may contact customers through email, phone, SMS, WhatsApp, or other available communication channels regarding orders, customer support, account-related matters, or service updates.

* Where legally required, promotional communications will be subject to applicable consent and opt-out requirements.

5. Cookies & Similar Technologies

* Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, security, analytics, preferences, and performance.

* You may be able to manage certain cookie preferences through your browser or device settings.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

* We may work with trusted service providers for payment processing, shipping, logistics, website hosting, analytics, communication, technology, customer support, and other business functions.

* Such service providers may process information only as necessary to provide the relevant services, subject to applicable agreements and laws.

7. Information Security

* We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organisational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

* However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

* We may retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, provide services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and comply with applicable legal requirements.

9. Children's Privacy

* Our website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

* Parents or legal guardians should supervise children's use of online services where appropriate.

10. Your Rights & Choices

* Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including requesting access, correction, updating, or other lawful requests.

* You may contact us to make a privacy-related request.

11. External Links

* Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. Dharatatva is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

* Dharatatva may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices, technology, services, or applicable law.

* Any updated version will be published on this page.

13. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, requests, or concerns, please contact:

Email: support@dharatatva.com

Note

By using the Dharatatva website, you acknowledge that you have reviewed this Privacy Policy and understand how information may be collected and processed as described above.
