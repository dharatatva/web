Shipping Policy

At Dharatatva, we aim to process and deliver your orders safely and efficiently. This Shipping Policy explains our general order processing, dispatch, delivery, and shipment procedures.

1. Order Processing

* Orders are generally processed after successful order confirmation and payment verification, where applicable.

* Processing time may vary depending on product availability, order volume, weekends, public holidays, promotional periods, and other operational circumstances.

2. Dispatch

* Once an order has been processed, it may be handed over to an authorised courier or logistics partner for delivery.

* Customers may receive shipping or tracking information where tracking is available for the selected shipment.

3. Delivery Time

* Estimated delivery timelines depend on the destination, courier network, service availability, weather, holidays, and other factors.

* Any delivery timeline displayed on the website is an estimate and should not be considered an absolute guarantee unless specifically stated otherwise.

4. Shipping Charges

* Shipping charges, if applicable, will be displayed during the checkout process or communicated before order confirmation.

* Shipping charges may vary depending on destination, order value, shipment weight, promotional offers, and other applicable factors.

5. Delivery Address

* Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate delivery address, including PIN code, contact number, and other information required for successful delivery.

* Dharatatva shall not be responsible for delays or failed deliveries resulting from incorrect, incomplete, or inaccessible delivery information provided by the customer.

6. Failed Delivery Attempts

* The courier partner may make one or more delivery attempts depending on its operational procedures.

* If delivery cannot be completed because the recipient is unavailable, the address is incorrect, the customer refuses delivery, or other circumstances prevent delivery, the shipment may be returned to Dharatatva.

* Additional delivery or re-shipping arrangements, where applicable, may be subject to the circumstances of the order.

7. Damaged or Tampered Packages

* Customers are encouraged to inspect the outer package at the time of delivery wherever reasonably possible.

* If a package appears significantly damaged, opened, leaking, or tampered with, customers should document the condition and contact Dharatatva Customer Support as soon as possible.

* Photographs or videos of the package and product may be requested for verification.

8. Lost or Delayed Shipments

* In the event of an unusual shipping delay, tracking issue, or suspected lost shipment, customers should contact Customer Support.

* Dharatatva may coordinate with the relevant logistics provider to investigate the shipment and determine the appropriate resolution.

9. Serviceability

* Delivery availability may depend on the serviceability of the customer's PIN code and the network of our logistics partners.

* Certain remote or restricted locations may have limited delivery availability or extended delivery timelines.

10. International Shipping

* Unless specifically stated on the website, Dharatatva orders may be intended for delivery within India.

* Where international shipping is offered, additional shipping charges, customs duties, taxes, import restrictions, documentation, and destination-country requirements may apply.

11. Force Majeure & Unavoidable Delays

* Dharatatva shall not be responsible for delivery delays caused by circumstances beyond its reasonable control, including severe weather, natural disasters, transportation disruptions, strikes, government restrictions, public emergencies, technical failures, or other unforeseen events.

12. Contact Us

For shipping, tracking, or delivery-related assistance, please contact:

Email: support@dharatatva.com

Important Note

Delivery timelines, shipping charges, and service availability may vary depending on the destination, courier network, order type, product availability, and other operational factors.
